# Content Integrity Rules

These rules safeguard the professional authenticity and accuracy of information presented in **alhassan-portfolio**.

## Absolute Truthfulness & No Hallucination
- **Zero Invention Policy**:
  - Never invent, extrapolate, or fabricate professional information, employment history, company names, job titles, start/end dates, education, certifications, metrics, or external links.
  - Never generate fake testimonials, client quotes, or placeholder project statistics (e.g. "increased revenue by 300%").
  - Official CV documents, verified portfolio notes, and explicit user-provided data are the sole sources of truth.
- **Handling Incomplete or Missing Data**:
  - If information is missing, ambiguous, or contradictory, ask the user directly for clarification instead of guessing or filling gaps with assumptions.
  - When staging new templates or sections before content is supplied, use explicit neutral structural placeholders clearly demarcated as `[Placeholder: Awaiting Verified Content]` rather than realistic-sounding fake claims.

## Editorial Quality & Voice
- **Web-First Copywriting**:
  - Do not blindly dump paragraphs of dense resume/CV prose onto the page.
  - Craft sharp, active, scannable web copy that highlights engineering craftsmanship, technical problems solved, and tangible impact.
  - Keep sentences concise, punchy, and confident without corporate jargon or hyperbole.

## Projects Showcase Standard
Every featured case study or project item must communicate structured, rigorous engineering detail whenever verified data exists:
- **Problem**: What was the real-world challenge, technical bottleneck, or opportunity?
- **Role**: What was Alhassan's specific responsibility, ownership, and scope?
- **Solution**: What architecture, algorithmic decisions, or design implementation solved it?
- **Technologies**: Exactly what stack, libraries, and tools were leveraged?
- **Result**: What was the verified outcome, performance improvement, user feedback, or deliverable?
