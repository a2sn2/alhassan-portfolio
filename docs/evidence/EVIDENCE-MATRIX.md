## 3. Governance Classifications

- **`EVIDENCE CONFIRMED` (`verified`)**: Source code repository, local source archive, or official certificate PDF is verified and published under public governance.
- **`PARTIAL`**: Core architectural components are isolated and verified, but supporting peripheral modules remain unseparated or partially preserved.
- **`SOURCE CONFLICT`**: Physical evidence indicates alternative implementation technologies relative to canonical CV claims. In accordance with Section A & M rules, canonical CV facts are strictly preserved and the discrepancy is flagged for owner review.
- **`MISSING`**: Physical artifact or repository is not available in the local evidence archive. No synthetic or placeholder files are generated.
- **`ONGOING`**: Program or diploma is actively in progress. No completion certificate is expected until formal program conclusion.
- **`PRIVATE`**: Academic transcripts, recommendation letters, grade reports, and third-party contact information are protected and excluded from public publication.
